# Annotation Guidelines

# Entity Types

Person (PER)
Label the names of people.

# Examples:
- John Doe
- Peter Joe
- Elon Musk

# Organization (ORG)
Label the names of companies, institutions, government agencies, schools, and organizations.

# Examples:
- Microsoft
- Access Bank
- University of Lagos
- United Nations

# Location (LOC)
Label countries, states, cities, towns, and other geographical locations.

# Examples:
- Nigeria
- Lagos
- Abuja
- London

---

### Date (DATE)
Label dates and years.

**Examples:**
- 15 July 2026
- Monday
- January 2025

# Money (MONEY)
Label amounts of money.

# Examples
- ₦150,000
- $250
- €500

# Email (EMAIL)
Label email addresses.

# Examples:
- john@example.com
- support@company.com

# Phone Number (PHONE)
Label phone numbers.

# Examples:
- +234 801 234 5678
- +918012345678
