# Annotation Guidelines

# Categories

Account Support
Assign this category to messages related to user accounts, passwords, login issues, or account settings.
# Examples:
- I forgot my password.
- I can't log into my account.

# Billing
Assign this category to messages related to payments, charges, invoices, refunds, or subscriptions.
# Examples:
- My payment failed.
- I was charged twice.

# Delivery
Assign this category to messages related to shipping, tracking, or delivery status.
# Examples:
- My package hasn't arrived.
- Where is my order?

# Returns
Assign this category to messages related to returning or exchanging products.
# Examples:
- I want to return my order.
- How do I exchange this item?
